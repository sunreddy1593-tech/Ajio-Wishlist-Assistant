## Brand & Style

This design system serves as an antidote to impulsive consumption and dark-pattern retail interfaces. It frames fashion purchasing not as a transactional checkout race, but as a considered, editorial curation process. The aesthetic combines the restrained authority of an archival print catalogue with the precision of a modern analytical decision-support tool.

### Visual Style
- **Restrained Editorial Minimalism:** Clean horizontal lines, generous rhythmic margins, and structural discipline replace flashy marketing banners and countdown timers.
- **Evidence-Led Clarity:** Every garment score, material analysis, and cost-per-wear breakdown is presented with neutral visual weight and systematic data tables.
- **Zero Artificial Urgency:** Flashing tags, "only 2 left" alerts, and aggressive strikethrough pricing are strictly omitted. Decisions are allowed breathing room.
- **Surface Integrity:** Solid, warm paper-tinted foundations with flat white layered sheets replace heavy dropshadows or skeuomorphic embellishments.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in archival neutrals, grounded by an authoritative Deep Burgundy primary accent, and accented by deliberate status colors calibrated for quiet clinical legibility. There is strictly no dark mode; the interface is conceived as an illuminated matte broadsheet.

### Canvas & Structure
- **Base Canvas:** `#F7F6F3` (Warm Alabaster) — Sets an intentional non-digital tone reminiscent of heavy linen paper.
- **Card / Sheet Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) — Dedicated to interactive modules, comparative tables, and item analyses.
- **Structural Hairlines:** `#E6E2DC` (Parchment Border) — 1px borders providing structure without visual noise.

### Typography & Content
- **Primary Ink:** `#171717` — Maximum contrast for editorial headlines, item titles, and primary data figures.
- **Secondary Ink:** `#626262` — Muted weight for metadata, wear-frequency calculations, and secondary supporting text.
- **Tertiary Ink:** `#8C887B` — Captions, disabled indicators, and structural grid rules.

### Purpose-Led Status & Analytical Accents
- **Primary Accent (Burgundy):** `#8B1E3F` | Tint: `#F8EDEF` — Decisive primary actions, selected curation states, key metrics.
- **Positive Status (Deep Green):** `#1F6B4F` | Tint: `#EAF5EF` — High wardrobe alignment, sustainable fiber score, verified durability.
- **Warning Status (Amber):** `#946200` | Tint: `#FFF4D6` — Redundant wardrobe duplicate, high synthetic content, dry-clean-only friction.
- **Comparison / Reference (Blue):** `#315C8C` | Tint: `#EAF1F8` — Comparative metrics, cross-wishlist matching, baseline standards.
- **Waiting / Deliberation (Slate):** `#596273` | Tint: `#EEF0F3` — 30-day cooling-off hold, seasonal pending, price surveillance.
- **Reconsider / Gap (Muted Red):** `#934646` | Tint: `#F8EAEA` — Poor value index, frequent return category, impulsive match.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes quiet readability and structural poise. `Manrope` governs display, titles, and body prose, offering a geometric yet warm humanist presence. `Inter` handles discrete labels, status tags, and tabular decision values where strict legibility and mono-like clarity are critical.

### Rules of Usage
- **Prose Minimum:** Core analysis body text must never drop below 15px (`body-md`) with at least 24px line height to encourage reflective reading.
- **Labels & Tags:** Rendered in `Inter` with slightly expanded tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) and uppercase transformation when indicating fixed status categorizations (e.g., `COST PER WEAR`, `HOLD ACTIVE`).
- **Data Uniformity:** Tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be enabled on all numerical data elements (fabric compositions, scores, price calculations) to maintain strict vertical alignment across comparative columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column grid system resting inside an expansive shell. White space is deliberate, acting as a cognitive separator that prevents the decision fatigue typical of e-commerce listings.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px outer page margins. Wishlist items collapse into single-column analytical units. Comparative matrices transform into swipeable side-by-side sheets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px outer page margins. Multi-item comparisons lock at 2 columns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, max-width 1152px centered within the `#F7F6F3` canvas. Accommodates a 3-way comparative matrix or a split-pane layout (Item Detail + Decision Scorecard).

### Rhythm
- Maintain a minimum of `space-2xl` (48px) vertical separation between independent functional blocks (e.g., between "Wardrobe Overlap Check" and "Long-Term Cost Estimate").
- Internal card padding is strictly uniform at 20px (`1.25rem`) on mobile and 24px (`1.5rem`) on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews realistic shadows, heavy drop-shadows, and blur effects. Depth is expressed purely through surface layering and high-precision hairline borders.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (`#F7F6F3`):** Canvas ground.
2. **Sheet Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority operational cards, evaluation panels, and data modules. Must be outlined with a crisp 1px border (`#E6E2DC`).
3. **Sub-Surface Layer (`#F8F7F5`):** Recessed calculation modules, data callout boxes, and material breakdowns nested inside white cards. No border or 1px dashed `#E6E2DC`.

### Focus and Active States
- **No Floating Elevation:** Hovering over an item card does not produce a lift effect. Instead, the 1px border transitions from `#E6E2DC` to a confident `#8B1E3F` (Burgundy) or neutral `#171717`.
- **Modals & Overlays:** When an item comparison modal opens, use a flat 40% `#171717` scrim. The overlay card uses pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px `#E6E2DC` border and a zero-blur keyline shadow: `0 4px 20px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The geometry is restrained and architectural. Surfaces use soft, tailored corners that convey craftsmanship without appearing overly playful or toy-like.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`), providing a clean frame that pairs comfortably with apparel photography.
- **Action Buttons & Form Inputs:** Set to `rounded-md` (8px / `0.5rem`) to maintain tactile certainty.
- **Badges, Status Pills & Tags:** Rendered as full pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to distinctly contrast against the rectangular structural cards.
- **Product Imagery Masks:** Match card roundedness (12px inner radius when padded, 16px when flush).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Commit / Proceed):** `#8B1E3F` solid fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-md` (8px), no shadows. Height: 44px (touch-compliant). Hover: `#751834`.
- **Secondary Action (Deliberate / Save to Cooling-Off):** `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E6E2DC` border, `#171717` text. Hover: `#F8EDEF` tint with `#8B1E3F` border.
- **Tertiary / Destructive Action (Discard from Wishlist):** Transparent fill, `#626262` text, underlined on hover. Zero visual aggression.

### Decision Badges & Chips
- Structure: 24px height, full pill (`rounded-full`), padded `space-xs` (8px) horizontally.
- Typographic style: `label-sm`, uppercase, font weight 600.
- Color Pairings:
  - *High Wardrobe Value:* `#1F6B4F` text on `#EAF5EF` background.
  - *Overlap Warning:* `#946200` text on `#FFF4D6` background.
  - *Cooling Off (Hold):* `#596273` text on `#EEF0F3` background.
  - *Reconsider:* `#934646` text on `#F8EAEA` background.
  - *Comparative Reference:* `#315C8C` text on `#EAF1F8` background.

### Decision Cards & Wishlist Items
- Always Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px `#E6E2DC` border and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Internal layout cleanly splits image, product metadata, and the **Evidence Block**:
  - Image: 3:4 aspect ratio, muted desaturated background placeholder.
  - Evidence Block: Displays cost-per-wear index, fiber breakdown (e.g., `100% Linen`), and redundancy check score.
  - Never display strikethrough "deal" prices or "X people looking at this" warnings.

### Comparison Matrix Table
- Alternating subtle horizontal dividers (`1px solid #E6E2DC`).
- Fixed primary column (Attributes: Material, Cost-per-wear target, Occasion overlap, Care friction).
- Parallel item columns allowing direct side-by-side objective evaluation.

### Form Inputs & Sliders
- Inputs: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E6E2DC` border, 8px radius, `#171717` text. Focus state: 1px `#8B1E3F` outline (no glowing blur).
- Wear Frequency Slider: Thin 2px `#E6E2DC` track with `#8B1E3F` filled progress, smooth circular 16px `#8B1E3F` thumb.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), `#FFFFFF` fill with 1.5px `#E6E2DC` border.
- Checked state: `#8B1E3F` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark/dot.
